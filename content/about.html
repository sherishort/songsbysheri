Songs written one at a time for the people and the moments that asked for them. That’s the truest way I know to describe what you’ll find here.

My very first song came in 2003, after my daddy passed. When I was a little girl I held his hand, and he was my hero. Before he went home, he told me I would be okay, because I didn’t need him the same old way. When life got hard, I needed the BIG Daddy. I needed God. That song, “Look to the Father,” is where all of this began.

“The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him.” Psalm 28:7

Since then, the songs have come out of real seasons of faith, of doubt, and of the moments God stepped in. Some are testimony. Some are praise and prayer. Many were written for the family and friends God gave me: my grandchildren, the people I love, and the ones I’ve had to let go. A few even came out of my years selling houses here in Ocala, Florida.

I write the words, and I pray they carry something that words alone can’t. Thank you for listening, and for letting these songs be a small part of your story too.
