Music written to carry what words alone can't

I write and record gospel and Christian music under the name Sheryl Denise Long. These songs came out of real seasons of faith, doubt, and the moments God stepped in. Some are testimony. Some are praise, prayer, and worship. Some were written for the family and friends God gave me, and a few came out of my days selling houses here in Ocala. Thank you for listening.
